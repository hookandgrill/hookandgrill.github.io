Fish Restaurant: Hook & Grill – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

This policy explains how Fish Restaurant: Hook & Grill (package name: com.hookandgrill.oyun) processes personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Controller and contact

Controller: Heron Games (independent developer). Contact: abdrakami@gmail.com. Please send any privacy request to this address.

2. In short

The game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email or location. We do not run any servers and no personal data from the game reaches us. Personal data is processed only by the Google services used in the game (Google Play Games and Google AdMob).

4. Ads and consent (Google AdMob)

The game shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland see Google's consent screen on first launch. Whether to allow personalized ads is your choice; if you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice or withdraw consent at any time in the game under Settings → Ad Preferences. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Google → Ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Data is shared only with Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC) through Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and may process it outside Europe, including in the United States. Such transfers are protected by the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

6. Retention

The save on your device is kept until you uninstall the app. The cloud save stays in your Google account until you delete it. Ad data is subject to Google's retention periods (Google data retention). We store no data ourselves.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and KVKK (Art. 11) you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold no data that can be linked to you, most requests are fulfilled directly through Google:

  • Save on your device: uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save and Play Games data: Google Play Games app settings or Google Account → Data & privacy.
  • Ad preferences: in the game, Settings → Ad Preferences; on your device, Settings → Google → Ads.

Contact us if you need help; we reply within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

8. Automated decision-making

The game makes no automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. If you consent, ad personalization is performed by Google.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. In some European countries the age of digital consent is 16; if you are younger, ask a parent before allowing personalized ads. If you believe a child's data has been processed, please contact us.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its update date.
